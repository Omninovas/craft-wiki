In VectorCraft, every shape, line and letter is a path: an outline that stays sharp at any size. The guides below start with a new document and end with automation, and you can read each one on its own.

Shortcuts are written for Windows and Linux first, then macOS: **Ctrl+G** (**Cmd+G** on macOS). You can change every shortcut in **Edit → Keyboard Shortcuts…** (**Ctrl+Alt+Shift+K**, or **Cmd+Option+Shift+K** on macOS). **Help → Search Commands…** (**Ctrl+Shift+/**, or **Cmd+Shift+/** on macOS) finds any menu command by name.

:::note
Some menu items are grayed out. Those features are not built yet; they are listed so you can see what is coming.
:::

## Find a tool

Each button in the Tools panel holds one tool or a group of tools. A button with a small triangle in its corner holds a group. Press and hold the button, or click the triangle, to choose another tool from the group. The default toolbar shows fewer groups. Choose **Window → Toolbars → Advanced** to show every group, in the order of the following table.

Press a tool's key to select it, even when its button is not showing. Tool keys are the same on every platform. You can give a tool without a key its own key in **Edit → Keyboard Shortcuts…**.

## Draw in perspective

Show the grid with **View → Perspective Grid → Show Grid** (**Ctrl+Shift+I**, or **Cmd+Shift+I** on macOS). Choose **One Point Perspective**, **Two Point Perspective** or **Three Point Perspective** from the same menu. While the grid is showing, the Rectangle, Ellipse, Polygon, Star and Line tools draw on its active plane. Use the **Perspective Selection Tool** (**Shift+V**) to move art along its plane.

![Two lit building facades drawn on the left and right planes of a two-point perspective grid at sunset, with the Perspective Selection tool active and the plane widget in the top-left corner](/assets/uploads/vectorcraft/perspective.webp)

[Draw in perspective](/apps/vectorcraft/draw-in-perspective/) covers adjusting the grid, choosing the active plane, putting flat art and type on a plane, and saving grid presets.

## Measure and dimension

The **Dimension Tool** adds lengths, angles and radii to technical drawings. Choose **Linear**, **Angular** or **Radial** in its bar, then drag out from an edge or click two points. The dimension follows the art when you change it. Set the units, precision and style in the **Properties** panel.

![The Lantern Bracket drawing, a lavender plate with a slot, two holes and a chamfered corner, dimensioned in coral millimeters on an off-white sheet. The Dimension Tool bar has Linear selected while a new 119.9 mm dimension is dragged up from the plate's top edge, and the Properties panel shows the Dimension section](/assets/uploads/vectorcraft/dimensions.webp)

[Measure and dimension](/apps/vectorcraft/measure-and-dimension/) covers the Measure tool, the Info panel, every dimension option and Document Info.

## Undo and the History panel

- **Edit → Undo** (**Ctrl+Z**, or **Cmd+Z** on macOS) reverses the last change, and **Edit → Redo** (**Ctrl+Shift+Z**, or **Cmd+Shift+Z** on macOS) makes it again. The menu item names the step, for example **Undo Move**. Undo and Redo are also at the top of the menu that opens when you right-click the canvas.
- **Window → History** lists the steps you can undo and redo, oldest first. The first row, **Open**, is the earliest state the panel can return to, and the highlighted row is the current state.
- Click a row to take the document back to that step. The steps after it turn gray, and you can click one of them to go forward again. When you make a new change, the gray steps are removed.
- The panel menu has **Step Backward**, **Step Forward**, **Clear History** and **Set History Limit…**. **Clear History** removes every step and leaves the artwork as it is; you cannot undo it.
- **Set History Limit…** opens the **Performance** page of Preferences. **History States** sets how many steps each document keeps, from 5 to 1000 (500 by default). When the steps use a lot of memory, VectorCraft removes the oldest ones first and keeps at least the last 8 steps.

![A Night Market sign with a coral and amber awning and three glowing bulbs on a string of lights. The History panel is docked on the right and lists Open, Pen, Fill Color, Stroke Color, Ellipse, Fill Color, Move and three Transform Again steps; the first Transform Again is highlighted and the two after it are gray](/assets/uploads/vectorcraft/workflows-history.webp)

[Customize the workspace](/apps/vectorcraft/customize-the-workspace/) covers docking the History panel and the other panels.

## Change how VectorCraft looks

- Choose **Edit → Preferences…** (**Ctrl+K**) and open the **User Interface** page. On macOS, choose **VectorCraft → Settings → User Interface…**. The page sets the **Brightness** (**Dark**, **Medium Dark**, **Medium Light** or **Light**), the **Accent Color**, the **Canvas Color** and the interface size under **UI Scaling**.
- **Language** on the same page changes the interface language. By default, VectorCraft follows your system's language.
- **Window → Workspace** switches between panel layouts and saves your own.
- [Preferences](/apps/vectorcraft/preferences/) lists every page of the dialog, and [Customize the workspace](/apps/vectorcraft/customize-the-workspace/) covers panels, workspaces and toolbars.
